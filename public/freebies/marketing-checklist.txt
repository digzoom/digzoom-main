DigZoom — Free marketing launch checklist
=========================================

1. Define one goal: enquiries, sales, or newsletter signups.
2. Describe your ideal customer and the problem you solve.
3. Write one clear offer with price, timing, and any conditions.
4. Prepare one landing page with a single action button.
5. Check the page on a phone and test the form yourself.
6. Prepare three variations of the headline and visual.
7. Add tagged links so you can see which channel works.
8. Start with a small budget only after checking the destination.
9. Record views, clicks, qualified enquiries, and actual sales.
10. Improve the page and message based on results before scaling.
